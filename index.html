Haggle — Shopping Negotiator
Simulated marketplace — seller bots. These listings and sellers are seeded demo data. This app cannot message real sellers on OLX, Facebook Marketplace or eBay, and it does not try to.

Find the price. Then haggle for it.

Pick a product, set the most you will pay, and let the negotiator compare listings and talk to three sellers at once. Every offer is checked against your budget in code — it cannot offer a rupee more.

Guardrails

What the agent cannot do

  • Offer above your hard budget — offers are clamped in code.
  • Accept above target + 5% — accepts outside that are blocked and logged.
  • Keep haggling past your max rounds — it walks away instead.
  • See or reveal a seller's lowest price — reservation prices stay hidden.

Seller replies: price ladder (no key). Add a free Gemini key in Settings for AI-written replies.

    Settings — Gemini API key

    Optional. Without a key, seller replies come from a fixed price ladder and the demo still works end to end. With a free Gemini key, replies are written by the model (gemini-2.5-flash) and then checked against the same rules — a reply below a seller's lowest price is clamped, and a reply with no usable price falls back to the ladder.

    The key is stored in this browser's localStorage only. It is sent only to Google's Gemini API, never to any other server. Get a free key at Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com).
